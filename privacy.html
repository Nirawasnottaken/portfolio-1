Privacy Policy & Data Protection.

This policy details the privacy practices for the static portfolio website of Niranjan (accessible at this domain). As an engineering student, I value user privacy, data minimization, and total transparency.

1. Zero Cookie Policy & No Consent Banner Needed

This website does not use any first-party or third-party cookies, local storage tracking identifiers, or session tokens. Because zero tracking or advertising cookies are utilized, no intrusive cookie consent banner is needed or displayed under EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR requirements.

2. Data Minimization & Form Handling

This website does not feature server-side form submissions, registration accounts, or database intake forms. When you click an email link or copy the email address, communications occur entirely peer-to-peer via your selected email provider. No intermediate servers intercept or log your message content.

3. Third-Party Auditing & Zero Tracking SDKs

This portfolio strictly avoids surveillance software:

  • Zero advertising pixels (e.g., Meta Pixel, TikTok pixel).
  • Zero behavioral tracking or heatmapping tools (e.g., Hotjar, FullStory).
  • Zero commercial analytics SDKs or fingerprinting scripts.
  • Google Fonts are loaded strictly via public CDN for typography styling with standard asset caching.

4. Hosting Infrastructure & Technical Server Logs

When you browse this site, the underlying web infrastructure host (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare) may automatically receive standard server request metadata, such as your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and request timestamp, solely for DDoS mitigation, routing, and delivery. This website has no access to or retention of these provider logs.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

This website is an engineering showcase intended for general professional audiences and does not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal data from children under the age of 16.

6. Data Subject Rights & Deletion Requests

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global data privacy standards, you maintain rights regarding your personal data. Because this portfolio does not collect or store user accounts, databases, or analytics records, there is no personal data stored to delete or alter. However, if you have previously sent an email and wish to request its permanent deletion from my inbox, please contact me directly.
